INDUSTRIAL MAINTENANCE & MACHINE REPAIR ACROSS FLORIDA

IDS supports manufacturers, food and beverage facilities, packaging operations and industrial production sites across Florida with machinery troubleshooting, preventive maintenance and repair.

Industrial maintenance throughout Florida

IDS provides industrial maintenance and machine troubleshooting for production facilities throughout Florida. Services include preventive and corrective maintenance, industrial machinery repair, mechanical troubleshooting, electrical diagnostics, PLC and controls support, VFD and servo troubleshooting, pneumatic and hydraulic systems, conveyors and automated production equipment.

Packaging machinery service

Packaging equipment is a core specialty, including VFFS machines, flow wrappers, stick pack equipment, filling and dosing systems, bagging equipment, conveyors, sensors, sealing systems and integrated packaging lines. We support food, candy, gummy, confectionery and other packaged-product operations.

Mechanical, electrical and automation support

Industrial downtime often crosses multiple systems. IDS can investigate motors, sensors, controls, PLC I/O, VFDs, servo systems, pneumatic cylinders and valves, hydraulic components, conveyors, machine timing, alignment and other equipment conditions affecting production.

Florida service coverage

We serve Central Florida and consider industrial service projects throughout the state based on scope, urgency and travel requirements, including the Orlando, Tampa Bay, Lakeland, Kissimmee, Davenport, Jacksonville, Gainesville, Ocala, Space Coast, Treasure Coast, Palm Beach, Fort Lauderdale, Miami and Southwest Florida markets.

Industries we support

Our services are intended for manufacturing plants, food and beverage production, candy and gummy production, packaging operations, warehouses with automated equipment, distribution facilities and other industrial environments.
