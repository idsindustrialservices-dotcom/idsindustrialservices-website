Choose a Stick Pack Machine for Your Product

Stick pack machines form narrow sachets from rollstock, fill the measured product and seal each pack. Equipment selection depends on the product, film, fill quantity, sealing requirements and output target. Contact IDS to discuss availability and an appropriate configuration; specific inventory and performance must be confirmed before quotation.

Specifications to Confirm

  • Product: powder, granules or compatible liquid, including flow behavior and bulk density.
  • Package: sachet width and length, seal type, film material and barrier requirements.
  • Dosing: target fill weight or volume and acceptable tolerance.
  • Capacity: number of lanes, target packs per minute and changeover requirements.
  • Installation: power, compressed air, footprint, feeding, coding and downstream equipment.

Single-Lane and Multi-Lane Applications

Lane count influences output, machine footprint, product distribution and integration. We can review the production goal before suggesting a suitable machine design. No specific speed or lane availability is guaranteed without a model-specific proposal.

Integrated Stick Pack Lines

Depending on project scope, stick pack solutions may require product feeding, dosing, date coding, conveying, counting and secondary packaging. Explore complete packaging lines or our broader packaging machines for sale.

Technical Service and Support

IDS also supports stick pack machine service and troubleshooting, industrial maintenance, electrical controls and packaging equipment integration. Service scope is confirmed for each project.
