Conveyor Problems We Troubleshoot

Conveyor issues can originate in mechanical components, motors, electrical circuits, sensors, controls or variable frequency drives. IDS works through the complete system to identify why material flow has slowed, stopped or become unreliable.

  • Conveyor will not start or stops unexpectedly
  • Belt tracking, slipping and mechanical movement problems
  • Motor and gearbox-related symptoms
  • VFD alarms and speed-control problems
  • Photo eyes, proximity sensors and accumulation logic
  • PLC sequence and interlock problems
  • Electrical and control-panel faults
  • Synchronization between conveyors and packaging machines

Conveyors in Packaging Lines

Packaging operations depend on reliable product infeed, transfer, accumulation and discharge. IDS supports conveyors connected to VFFS machines, flow wrappers, stick pack machines and filling and dosing equipment.

Controls and Drive Diagnostics

If the problem involves machine sequencing, sensors or communications, see our PLC and controls troubleshooting. Motor-speed and drive faults can be handled through servo and VFD troubleshooting.

Preventive Conveyor Maintenance

Regular inspections can help identify wear, alignment issues, looseness, abnormal noise and recurring sensor or drive problems before a production stoppage. Learn more about industrial preventive maintenance.
