Filling and Dosing Troubleshooting

Inconsistent fill, timing errors, product-feed problems or machine stops can involve mechanical components, sensors, controls, pneumatics, servo systems or upstream/downstream equipment. IDS investigates the complete machine function instead of treating every symptom as an isolated component failure.

Common Problems We Investigate

  • Inconsistent filling or dosing cycles
  • Machine timing and synchronization problems
  • Sensor, photo-eye and I/O faults
  • PLC sequence and interlock issues
  • Servo and VFD alarms
  • Pneumatic valves, cylinders and actuators
  • Electrical and control-panel faults
  • Integration with conveyors and packaging equipment

Integration with Packaging Machinery

Filling and dosing systems often work as part of a larger packaging line. IDS can troubleshoot interactions with VFFS machines, stick pack equipment, flow wrappers and industrial conveyors.

Controls, Drives and Pneumatics

When fill performance is affected by machine logic or control signals, see our PLC and controls troubleshooting. For motor-control issues, visit servo and VFD troubleshooting. Pneumatic and hydraulic support is also available for machine actuators and connected systems.
