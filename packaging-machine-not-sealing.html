Why Packaging Machines Develop Seal Problems

Poor seals can be related to temperature, dwell time, pressure, contaminated seal areas, film tracking, worn sealing components, heater or thermocouple problems, pneumatic pressure, machine timing or control conditions.

Mechanical, Electrical & Controls Diagnosis

A sealing problem is not always a sealing-jaw problem. IDS can evaluate mechanical alignment, heaters, temperature feedback, sensors, PLC sequences, pneumatics and drive timing to identify the condition affecting package quality.
