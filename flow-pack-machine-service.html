Flow Pack Problems We Troubleshoot

Flow wrappers combine film handling, product infeed, sealing, cutting, sensors, controls and motion systems. IDS works through these systems together when poor package quality or repeated machine stops affect production.

  • Film feed and tracking problems
  • Longitudinal and end-seal problems
  • Cutting and jaw timing issues
  • Product infeed and spacing problems
  • Registration mark and photo-eye faults
  • Machine timing and synchronization
  • PLC, sensor and I/O faults
  • Servo and VFD alarms
  • Pneumatic and mechanical functions

Film, Sealing & Registration

Inconsistent seals or film movement can involve alignment, temperature, pressure, timing, print registration, sensor setup or mechanical wear. IDS evaluates the full machine function instead of assuming the visible symptom identifies the failed component.

Product Infeed & Conveyor Synchronization

Flow wrappers depend on consistent product spacing and synchronization with upstream and downstream conveyors. Problems with sensors, speeds, accumulation or timing can create jams, missed products or package-quality issues.

PLC, Drives & Electrical Systems

IDS provides PLC and controls troubleshooting, servo and VFD diagnostics and industrial electrical troubleshooting when the flow pack problem involves automation, motion or electrical systems.

Preventive Flow Wrapper Maintenance

Planned inspection of belts, rollers, sealing assemblies, sensors, drives, pneumatics and recurring trouble areas can help reduce unexpected downtime. Learn more about preventive maintenance.
