Gummy & Candy Packaging Machines

IDS can help customers evaluate packaging equipment for gummies, gummy candy, confectionery and similar products. Depending on the product and package format, a solution may combine weighing or counting, product feeding, filling, sealing, conveying and downstream packaging equipment.

Food Packaging Machines

Packaging requirements vary by product, package material, target speed, fill weight and production environment. Equipment configurations may include automatic packaging machines, bagging machines, pouch packaging systems and integrated food packaging lines.

VFFS Packaging Machines

Vertical form-fill-seal equipment forms packages from rollstock, fills product and seals the package in an automated process. IDS also provides VFFS machine service and troubleshooting after installation.

Flow Pack & Horizontal Packaging

Horizontal flow wrapping can be used for individually wrapped or continuously fed products. See our flow pack service capabilities.

Filling, Dosing & Weighing Systems

Packaging lines can integrate filling machines, dosing equipment, multihead weighers, counters and product-feeding systems. Machine selection should be based on the actual product, target package, accuracy and required throughput.

Premade Pouch & Bagging Equipment

Premade pouch packaging and automated bagging solutions can be configured around product characteristics, pouch dimensions, closure requirements and production goals.

Complete Packaging Lines

Customers planning a larger automation project may need conveyors, weighing or counting, primary packaging, controls and downstream equipment integrated into a complete packaging line.
