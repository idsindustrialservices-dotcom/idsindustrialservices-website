Stick Pack Machine Troubleshooting

IDS provides technical troubleshooting and maintenance support for stick pack packaging equipment used in automated production environments. We can investigate mechanical, electrical, pneumatic and controls-related symptoms affecting machine operation, package quality or production consistency.

Stick Pack Machine Problems We Troubleshoot

  • Dosing and filling inconsistencies
  • Film feeding, tracking and package formation problems
  • Cutting, sealing and synchronization issues
  • Machine timing and indexing problems
  • Sensor, photo-eye, I/O and control faults
  • PLC sequence and interlock problems
  • Servo motor, drive and VFD alarms
  • Pneumatic cylinders, valves, pressure and actuator issues
  • Mechanical adjustment, wear and alignment problems

Dosing, Film & Package Formation

Stick pack performance depends on the interaction between the product dosing system, film path, forming components, sealing functions and machine timing. When package weight, seal quality, film tracking or cycle timing changes, IDS can evaluate the connected machine functions instead of treating one component in isolation.

Controls, Sensors & Motion

Modern stick pack equipment relies on sensors, PLC logic, motion control and drive systems to coordinate feeding, dosing, sealing and cutting. IDS can combine PLC and controls troubleshooting, servo and VFD diagnostics and industrial electrical troubleshooting when the symptom crosses multiple systems.

What to Send Before Service

For a faster initial assessment, send the machine manufacturer and model, alarm or fault code, a description of the problem, what changed before the issue started, facility location and photos or video when available. If production is stopped, use our emergency industrial service page.
