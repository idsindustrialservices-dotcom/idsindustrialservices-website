Reduce Unplanned Downtime

Preventive maintenance is intended to find developing problems before they become production failures. IDS can help inspect machine condition, identify wear or abnormal operation and prioritize corrective work around your production schedule.

Preventive Maintenance Areas

  • Mechanical inspection and machine functions
  • Electrical connections and control-panel condition
  • Sensors, switches and I/O checks
  • Servo and VFD alarm history and operating condition
  • Pneumatic leaks, valves, cylinders and regulators
  • Hydraulic components and visible leakage
  • Conveyors, drives and production-line components
  • Packaging machinery condition and recurring trouble areas

Packaging Machinery Preventive Maintenance

On packaging equipment, small issues with film handling, sealing assemblies, sensors, belts, drives or pneumatics can develop into repeated stops or product-quality problems. Planned checks can help address these conditions before they cause a larger interruption.

From Inspection to Corrective Action

When a developing fault is identified, IDS can connect preventive work with industrial maintenance, electrical troubleshooting, PLC troubleshooting or servo and VFD diagnostics.
