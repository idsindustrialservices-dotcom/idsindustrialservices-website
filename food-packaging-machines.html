Automated Food Packaging Equipment

Food packaging projects can combine product handling, weighing, filling, dosing, bagging, pouch packaging, flow wrapping, sealing and conveying. The right configuration depends on the product, package format, target output and available floor space.

VFFS, Pouches & Bagging

Vertical form-fill-seal and pouch systems can automate package forming or handling, filling and sealing. See our VFFS technical service.

Flow Wrapping

Horizontal flow pack systems can package continuously fed products and integrate with conveyors and controls. IDS also provides flow wrapper troubleshooting.

Filling, Dosing & Weighing

Filling machines, dosing systems, counters and multihead weighers can be integrated according to product characteristics and required accuracy.

Integrated Food Packaging Lines

For projects that require several operations in sequence, IDS can discuss complete packaging lines that connect feeding, weighing or dosing, bagging or flow wrapping, conveyors and downstream equipment. If cartonizing or another secondary-packaging step is part of the project, include it in the line requirements so the complete configuration can be evaluated.

COMMON PACKAGING EQUIPMENT QUESTIONS

What food packaging machines does IDS offer?

Packaging configurations can include VFFS, bagging, pouch packaging, filling, dosing, weighing and integrated conveyor systems, depending on the product and package requirements.

How do I choose a food packaging machine?

Machine selection depends on the product, package material and dimensions, target weight, production rate, accuracy requirements and available space.

Does IDS provide service for food packaging equipment?

Yes. IDS provides packaging machinery service and troubleshooting for automated food packaging equipment and connected production systems.

Can IDS help with an integrated food packaging line?

IDS can discuss complete packaging-line configurations that connect feeding, weighing or dosing, bagging or flow wrapping, conveyors and downstream equipment based on the project requirements.
