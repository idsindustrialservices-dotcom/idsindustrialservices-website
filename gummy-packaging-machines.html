Gummy Packaging Equipment for Automated Production

Gummy packaging equipment can combine product feeding, weighing or counting, filling, bagging or pouch handling, sealing and conveying. Equipment selection depends on gummy characteristics, target weight or count, package format and production speed.

Gummy Bag & Pouch Packaging

Solutions may include VFFS bagging or premade pouch equipment integrated with weighing, counting or dosing systems. Package dimensions, film or pouch material and seal requirements should be defined before machine selection.

Multihead Weighing, Counting & Filling

Depending on the product and target pack, a line may use a multihead weigher, counting system or other filling method. IDS can discuss the complete packaging process rather than only the primary bagger, including integrated packaging-line requirements.
