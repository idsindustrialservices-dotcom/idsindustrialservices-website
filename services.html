Industrial Maintenance

Our industrial maintenance service covers preventive and corrective work focused on equipment reliability, production continuity and reducing unplanned downtime.

Packaging Machinery Repair

Packaging equipment is a core IDS specialty. We troubleshoot VFFS machines, flow wrappers, stick pack machines, filling and dosing equipment, conveyors and connected production-line systems.

Packaging Machines for Sale

IDS also supports equipment projects for packaging machines for sale, including gummy and candy packaging, food packaging equipment and complete packaging lines.

PLC & Controls

PLC troubleshooting can include sensors, I/O, machine sequences, interlocks, control panels and communication problems affecting automated equipment.

Servo & VFD Drives

Servo and VFD troubleshooting includes drive alarms, parameter review, motor-control faults, feedback problems and synchronization issues.

Industrial Electrical Troubleshooting

Industrial electrical troubleshooting covers machine circuits, control panels, motors, sensors, relays, power supplies and electrical conditions that affect production equipment.

Pneumatics & Hydraulics

IDS troubleshoots valves, cylinders, actuators, regulators, leaks and hydraulic components that affect machine function. Learn more about pneumatic and hydraulic service.

Preventive Maintenance

Preventive maintenance can help identify wear, abnormal operation and recurring trouble areas before they become larger production failures.

Emergency Machine-Down Support

If production has stopped, use our emergency industrial service page and send the machine manufacturer, model, alarm code and symptoms when possible.

Serving Orlando & Central Florida

IDS is based in Orlando and serves industrial customers throughout Central Florida, including Kissimmee, Davenport, Lakeland and surrounding manufacturing areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers about IDS industrial maintenance and packaging machinery service.

What types of industrial maintenance does IDS provide?

IDS provides preventive and corrective industrial maintenance, troubleshooting and technical support for mechanical, electrical, PLC, servo, VFD, pneumatic and hydraulic systems.

Does IDS service packaging machinery?

Yes. IDS provides packaging machinery service and troubleshooting for VFFS, flow pack, stick pack, filling and dosing equipment, conveyors and integrated packaging lines.

Where does IDS provide industrial maintenance service?

IDS is based in Orlando, Florida and serves industrial customers throughout Central Florida, including Orlando, Kissimmee, Davenport, Clermont, Haines City, Ocoee and Lakeland.

Can IDS help when production is stopped?

Yes. For machine-down situations, contact IDS with the equipment manufacturer, model, alarm or fault code, symptoms and facility location so the team can begin an initial assessment.
