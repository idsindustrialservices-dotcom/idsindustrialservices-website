Industrial Maintenance in Lakeland

IDS supports businesses that depend on reliable industrial equipment, automated machinery and packaging systems. Our work can include preventive maintenance, corrective troubleshooting and technical support across mechanical, electrical and controls systems.

Equipment Troubleshooting

We support industrial electrical troubleshooting, PLC and controls, servo and VFD systems, pneumatic and hydraulic equipment, conveyors and related machine components.

Packaging Machinery Support

Packaging machinery is a core IDS specialty. We provide service and troubleshooting for VFFS machines, flow pack equipment, stick pack machines, filling and dosing systems and connected packaging lines.

Preventive Maintenance

Planned maintenance can help identify wear, recurring faults and developing equipment problems before they interrupt production. See our preventive maintenance services.

Serving Lakeland and Central Florida

IDS supports Lakeland from its Central Florida service operation and supports industrial customers throughout Central Florida based on project scope, urgency and travel requirements. If production is stopped, use our emergency industrial service page.
