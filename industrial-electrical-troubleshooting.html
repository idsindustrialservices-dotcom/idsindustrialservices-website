Electrical Problems on Industrial Machinery

Electrical faults can stop a machine completely or create intermittent problems that are difficult to reproduce. IDS uses the machine symptoms, electrical drawings when available, meter readings and control-system information to narrow the problem to the affected circuit or component.

Areas We Troubleshoot

  • Control panels, relays, contactors and power supplies
  • Machine wiring, loose connections and circuit faults
  • Motors and motor-control circuits
  • Safety circuits, interlocks and emergency-stop conditions
  • Sensors, switches and machine I/O
  • Electrical faults associated with PLC systems
  • Servo and VFD electrical issues
  • Packaging machinery electrical problems

Electrical, PLC and Drive Systems Work Together

Modern production equipment combines electrical power, PLC and controls, sensors and servo/VFD drives. A machine fault that looks electrical may be caused by a control signal, failed sensor, drive alarm or mechanical condition, so troubleshooting should consider the complete machine instead of replacing parts by guesswork.

Packaging and Production Equipment

IDS supports electrical troubleshooting on packaging machinery, conveyors, filling and dosing systems and other automated production equipment. When the machine is down and production is affected, use our emergency industrial service page.
