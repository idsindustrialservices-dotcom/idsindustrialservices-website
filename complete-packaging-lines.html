Complete & Integrated Packaging Lines

A complete packaging line may connect product feeding, weighing or counting, filling, primary packaging, conveyors, controls and downstream equipment into one production process. IDS can help evaluate packaging line equipment and how each machine must hand off product to the next stage.

Packaging Line Integration & Conveyor Systems

Successful packaging line integration requires attention to production rate, machine handoffs, accumulation, sensors, controls, electrical requirements, available floor space and upstream/downstream equipment. Conveyor systems are a key part of line integration because they control product transfer, buffering and synchronization between machines.

Primary Packaging Equipment

Line configurations can incorporate VFFS machines, flow wrappers, premade pouch equipment, filling and dosing systems and other packaging machinery according to the application.

Packaging Line Service & Maintenance

IDS combines packaging equipment knowledge with industrial maintenance, PLC and controls, drives, electrical troubleshooting, pneumatics and conveyor service. After installation, our team can also support troubleshooting and maintenance for the connected production line.

Packaging Production Line Planning

For a new or expanded packaging production line, useful planning details include the product, package format, desired line speed, upstream process, downstream packaging, conveyor requirements, utilities and available floor space.

COMMON PACKAGING EQUIPMENT QUESTIONS

What is included in a complete packaging line?

A complete line can integrate product feeding, weighing or counting, filling, primary packaging, conveyors, controls and downstream equipment based on the production requirements.

Can IDS help with packaging line integration?

Yes. IDS can discuss equipment requirements and how packaging machines, conveyors and controls need to work together as a production system.

What information is needed for a packaging line project?

Useful information includes the product, package format, target speed, fill weight or count, existing equipment, electrical requirements and available floor space.
